# Blog drafts

Published blog: https://blogs.gnome.org/tsaunier/ (WordPress on GNOME infrastructure),
aggregated on https://planet.igalia.com/ via its feed.

- `published/`: imported copies of live posts (from the RSS feed, converted to markdown).
- `drafts/`: work in progress. Move to `published/` once live.

Posts are submitted by pasting into the WordPress editor; nothing here is auto-published.
