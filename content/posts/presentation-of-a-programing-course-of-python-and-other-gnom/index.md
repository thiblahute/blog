<div style="text-align: justify;">

First I want to share with you the great tool I found to make presentations which is [Inskape](http://www.inkscape.org/) with the [JessyInk](http://code.google.com/p/jessyink/) plug-in. It lets you make presentations in SVG format quite easily, and I personally find the result pretty satisfying! I am using it for all my presentations now, and obviously for the ones I am making for this lesson. [Here](http://jessyink.googlecode.com/files/JessyInk_1_4_0_showcase.svg) is a good introduction to jessyInk, I also found a good [video tutorial](http://screencasters.heathenx.org/tag/jessyink/) to help you starting with this plug-in. I actually didn't know anything about Inkscape before this, but with this video tutorial I learned fast and I am now able to make presentations with those tools.
